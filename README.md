**What I'm working on**

- [quant-research-system](https://github.com/lUIZhMACEDO/quant-research-system) — end-of-day equity
  research pipeline. 5 years of prices and 16 FRED series in SQLite, indicators including a Hurst
  exponent regime read, five strategy screens, a Streamlit dashboard, and an emailed report every
  weekday. [Live app](https://ai-stock-analyst-1.streamlit.app)
- [trading-bot-ml](https://github.com/lUIZhMACEDO/trading-bot-ml) — paper-trading engine on Alpaca.
  LightGBM / random forest / Ridge ensemble trained walk-forward on 21-day forward-return direction,
  a backtester that charges costs and slippage, and a FastAPI dashboard.
- Soccer: a foul-won probability model on StatsBomb Premier League event data (calibrated XGBoost,
  match-level splits), and a Tableau dashboard on Premier League injury risk by position, age and
  height.
